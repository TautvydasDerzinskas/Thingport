# <img src="../assets/providers/fab365.svg" alt="" width="36" height="36" align="absmiddle"> Fab365

A South Korean 3D-model marketplace, at [fab365.net](https://fab365.net), running since 2018 and known for
meticulously engineered models -- SpaceX rockets, foldable robots, Star Wars ships -- most designed to print cleanly
without supports. Its model pages are anyone's to read; their files go only to a signed-in member who owns the model,
the free ones included, and your favourites are yours alone to see.

| At a glance    |                                                                                                                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sign-in        | Your own Fab365 sign-in, per user, for the files and your favourites                                             |
| Thingport Grab | Recommended -- it sends your sign-in as you visit fab365.net                                                     |
| FlareSolverr   | Not used                                                                                                         |
| Imports        | Free models and the ones you own; your favourites, [synced](../guides/synced-collections.md) too; its categories |

## How it works

Fab365's pages are built on a JSON API of its own, which is what Thingport reads.

- **Models.** Paste a model link (`https://fab365.net/items/<number>`) in Thingport, or import it from the model page
  with Grab. A model is one print of the zip Fab365 hands over, unpacked into its files, with the description, the
  whole gallery and the designer from its page.
- **Free models.** They still need your sign-in: Fab365 hands its files only to a signed-in member. There's no order
  to place -- a free model downloads straight away.
- **Paid and ticket models.** Thingport imports a model you already own -- bought, or redeemed with a Fab365 ticket.
  One you don't own yet is left for you to buy or redeem on fab365.net first; Thingport never spends your money or
  tickets.
- **Favourites.** Your [favourites](https://fab365.net/my/likes) are Fab365's only list, and only you can see them, so
  Thingport reads them with your sign-in. Import them in one go, or sync them so new ones follow.
- **Categories.** A model's page names its Fab365 category, which Thingport maps to one of yours.
- **Browse tab.** With your sign-in, the sync dialog lists your favourites.
- **Trending.** The dashboard's panel shows Fab365's most-liked models.

## Setup: your sign-in

Per-user (**Configuration → Providers → Fab365**). Fab365 keeps you signed in with a short-lived access token (a JWT)
in its page's local storage, with a refresh token beside it. Thingport keeps both and renews the access token itself as
it runs out.

### Easiest: Thingport Grab

With [Thingport Grab](../../extension/README.md) allowed on Fab365, opening fab365.net while you're signed in connects
your account, and sends a new sign-in whenever yours changes there.

### Manual: copy it from your browser

1. Sign in at [fab365.net](https://fab365.net).
2. Open DevTools (`F12`, or `Cmd+Option+I` on macOS).
3. Chrome/Edge: **Application** tab > **Local storage** > `https://fab365.net`. Firefox: **Storage** tab > **Local
   storage**.
4. Copy the values of `accessToken` and `refreshToken` one at a time, and put each on a line of its own after its name:

   ```text
   accessToken: eyJ0eXAi…
   refreshToken: eyJ0eXAi…
   ```

   The table's rows copied as text work too, if your browser lets you select them together.

5. Right-click **Fab365** in **Configuration → Providers**, choose **Connect** and paste them. With the refresh token,
   Thingport renews the sign-in itself. The `accessToken` value pasted on its own works too, but stops working after
   about 100 minutes and has to be pasted again.

Renewing doesn't sign you out of fab365.net: the site lets the same refresh token be used again, so your browser and
Thingport each keep their sign-in.
