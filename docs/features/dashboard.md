# Dashboard

The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in: an overview of your library at a glance.

- **Counts:** models, collections, unique authors, and categories and folders.
- **Recently added** models.
- **Top viewed** and **top printed** models. A model counts as printed when you download it or open it in a slicer.
- **Top authors** and **top providers**, by how many of their models you have.
- **Featured model:** a different model each day, in 3D, to spin with the mouse or click to open.
- **Clock** and **weather**, for a place you choose or your browser's location.
- **RSS feeds:** the newest items of the feeds you list, together. An item opens in a new tab.
- **Uptime:** whether each address you list answers, and how quickly. Admins can also show how long Thingport and
  the machine it runs on have been up.
- **CPU** (admins only): the server's CPU load with a short history, its load average and memory.
- **Launcher:** your bookmarks as app icons, each opening in a new tab. Give an item an icon by its image address, or
  leave it empty for the site's own icon. Icons can show in their own colours, mono or grayed; a search box and a limit
  with **Show all** are optional. It starts with Thingport's website and repository, which you can remove.

Feeds and addresses are fetched by the Thingport server, so they can be on your local network, like a printer or a
NAS. Only `http` and `https` addresses are fetched.

## Make it your own

The dashboard is a grid of panels you arrange yourself. **Edit** (the pencil above the panels) shows the grid's cells:

- **Move** a panel by dragging it, and **resize** it from its bottom-right corner. Without a mouse, focus a panel and use
  the arrow keys to move it, or Shift and the arrow keys to resize it.
- **Tabs:** drag a panel's name onto another panel to show the two as tabs in one, or select several panels and choose
  **Merge into tabs**. Drag a tab onto empty space, or choose **Split into panels**, to give it a panel of its own again.
- **Add panel** puts back any panel that isn't showing; **Remove** takes the selected ones off.
- **Settings** (the cog on a panel) changes what it shows: a 12- or 24-hour clock with or without the date and
  greeting, the weather in °C or °F with or without the later forecast, how many items a list shows (3, 5, 7 or 10)
  and whether it links to the full list, the feeds an RSS panel reads, the addresses an Uptime panel checks and a
  Launcher's links, which you drag into order. The panel resizes to fit; **Fit to content** in a panel's menu does the same
  after resizing by hand. A list too long for its panel scrolls under its title, which stays in place.

**Templates** (top right of the dashboard) switches between whole dashboards, each with its own panels and arrangement.
It comes with a few to start from -- Classic, Overview, Compact tabs, Puzzle and Minimal -- and **Manage templates**
makes new ones (empty, a copy of the current one or a built-in), renames, copies, resets or deletes them.

Wide, medium and phone-sized screens each keep their own arrangement: until you arrange one yourself, it follows the
wide one. Everything is saved to your account, so it follows you to other devices.
