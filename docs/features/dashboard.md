# Dashboard

The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in: an overview of your library at a glance.

- **Counts:** models, collections, unique authors, and categories and folders.
- **Recently added** models.
- **Top viewed** and **top printed** models. A model counts as printed when you download it or open it in a slicer.
- **Top authors** and **top providers**, by how many of their models you have.
- **Featured model:** a different model each day, in 3D, to spin with the mouse or click to open.
- **Clock** and **weather**, for a place you choose or your browser's location.

## Make it your own

**Customize dashboard** chooses which boxes are shown, how they're laid out (counts on the left, right, top or bottom,
in two or three columns, or a puzzle layout), and whether the lists show photos, monochrome pictures or names only.
The choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to other devices.
