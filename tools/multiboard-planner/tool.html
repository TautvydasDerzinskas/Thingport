Enter your wall size, pick a tile size, a tile setup and a mounting method. The planner fills the wall with full tiles, adds smaller tiles along the right and top edges, and lists every part.

  • Single tiles: bordered tiles joined by dual clips.
  • Grid tiles: interlocking core, edge and corner tiles (strips for a single row or column).
  • Snap colours: yellow single, orange dual, red quad.

The layout and counting rules are a port of the official Multiboard Planner (multiboard.itch.io/planner). Each part opens from your Tool: Multiboard models collection once Thingport has it, or on Thangs until then.
